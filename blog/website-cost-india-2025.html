Website Banwane Ka Kitna Kharcha Aata Hai? (Honest Guide — Updated 2026)

📅 28 May 2025 · Updated Jul 2026 ⏱ 9 min read ✍️ DigiRise India 🌍 PAN India

⚡ Seedha jawab: India mein ek professional business website ka kharcha ₹7,999 se ₹75,000+ tak jaata hai — depend karta hai aapko kya chahiye. Is guide mein hum bina jargon ke batayenge ki kaunsa option kiske liye sahi hai, hidden costs kya hote hain, aur paisa kahan waste hota hai.

Sabse Pehle: Website Ke 4 Price Brackets Samjho

Jab bhi koi bolta hai "website banwani hai", actual mein woh 4 alag-alag cheezein ho sakti hain. Har bracket ka apna use-case hai:

₹0–3k
DIY builders (Wix/Canva sites) — khud banao, time lagao
₹8k–15k
Professional starter site — 5-7 pages, mobile-first, SEO basics
₹25k–75k
Growth website — custom design, speed, conversion-focused

Option 1: Khud Banao (₹0 – ₹3,000/saal)

Wix, Google Sites, ya Canva Websites jaise DIY tools se aap free ya bahut kam kharche mein site bana sakte ho. Sach yeh hai: yeh option tabhi sahi hai jab aapke paas time ho, design sense ho, aur business abhi testing phase mein ho.

Hidden costs: custom domain (₹700–1,200/saal), ads hatane ke liye paid plan (₹1,500–3,500/saal), aur sabse bada — aapka time. 20-30 ghante aaram se lag jaate hain, aur result phir bhi "template jaisa" dikhta hai.

Option 2: Freelancer Se Banwao (₹5,000 – ₹20,000)

Freelancer sasta padta hai, lekin quality ka lottery system hai. Kabhi bahut accha mil jaata hai, kabhi aadha kaam karke gayab. Yeh dekho hire karne se pehle: live websites ka portfolio (screenshots nahi), mobile speed test, aur delivery ke baad support ka commitment — written mein.

Detail comparison ke liye padho: Agency vs Freelancer — kya sahi hai aapke liye?

Hidden Costs Jo Koi Nahi Batata

Website ki quoted price ke alawa yeh recurring kharche hote hain — inhe budget mein pehle se rakho:

Domain (₹700–1,200/saal) + hosting (₹0 static sites pe, ₹3,000–8,000/saal WordPress pe) + SSL (aajkal mostly free) + maintenance (updates, backups, security — DIY pe free lekin aapka time, agency pe ₹1,000–3,000/mahina optional). Static HTML websites (jaise hum banate hain) Vercel/Cloudflare pe ₹0 hosting pe chalti hain — yeh long-term sabse bada saving hai.

Paisa Kahan Waste Hota Hai? (3 Common Mistakes)

1. Design pe sab, speed pe kuch nahi. Heavy sliders aur animations waali site 4G pe 8-10 second mein khulti hai — customer wait nahi karta. Speed test karo launch se pehle: free Website Speed Checker.

2. Website hai, lekin Google pe nahi. Bina basic SEO aur Google Business Profile ke website ek digital visiting card hai jo kisi ne dekha nahi.

3. No clear CTA. Website ka ek hi kaam hai — visitor se action karwana (call, WhatsApp, enquiry). Agar homepage pe 10 second mein samajh nahi aata ki kya karna hai, redesign chahiye.

Final Verdict: Aapke Liye Kya Sahi Hai?

🎯 Testing phase / side hustle: DIY builder — ₹0-3k.
Serious local business (salon, clinic, store): Professional starter — ₹8-15k range.
Leads aur growth chahiye: Growth/Pro tier — ₹25-35k, conversion-focused.
Brand + scale: Elite tier ya custom — ₹75k+.

Sabse important: website ek investment hai, expense nahi — lekin sirf tab jab woh leads laaye. Price se zyada yeh poocho: "Yeh website mere liye customers kaise laayegi?"
