## 🏗️ Deep Audit & Structural Refinements

Every single file in this repository has been audited and upgraded to adhere to strict APEX protocols.

- **`index.html`**: Reduced base64 bloat (shrunk from 1.58MB to 365KB). Integrated Content-Security-Policy (CSP). Removed phantom schemas. Perfected AEO/GEO metadata.
- **`ios26.css` & `nextgen.css`**: Completely overhauled with the Midnight Gold palette (`#080604` / `#f7f2e9`). Added 3D tilt geometry, conic borders, scroll-fill timelines, and glassmorphism tiers.
- **`ios26.js` & `nextgen.js`**: Re-engineered progressive enhancement engine. Zero dependencies. Features View Transitions API, Intersection Observers for 0→fill animations, and strict `requestAnimationFrame` syncing for 60fps physics.
- **`sw.js` & `manifest.json`**: Upgraded to v3 caching strategies for full PWA offline support, asset pre-fetching, and fast-load states.
- **SEO & AI Suite**: Added `feed.xml` (RSS), `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, and `ai.txt` to ensure perfect discoverability by traditional bots (Google) and Next-Gen AI crawlers (Claude, GPT).
